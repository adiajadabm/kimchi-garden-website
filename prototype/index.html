Good to know

Questions?

How does ordering work?

Order and pay at a self-serve kiosk when you walk in, then pick up your food at the counter when your number is called. Water and utensils are self-serve.

How late are you open?

Until 12:30 AM most nights and 1:30 AM on Thursdays. On Sundays we take a break from 3:30 to 5 PM. See the full hours above.

Do you have vegan or vegetarian options?

Yes. The menu marks the vegetarian picks: fried seaweed rolls and waffle fries (both vegan), cheese sticks, vegetable kimbap, vegetable dumplings and the tofu mayo bowl. The fruit slushes are non-dairy. Ask at the counter about anything else.

Do you take reservations for groups?

Yes. Kimchi Garden is great for groups. Call ahead for big parties so we can save you a few tables.

Do you serve alcohol?

We serve beer, soju and cocktails. Bring a valid ID.
